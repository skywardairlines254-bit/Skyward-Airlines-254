Privacy Policy

Skyward Airlines collects the information you provide when making a booking enquiry, contacting us, or subscribing to our newsletter — including your name, contact details and travel preferences.

How we use your data

We use your information to process booking enquiries, respond to messages, and — where you've opted in — send route and fare updates.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with parties directly involved in fulfilling your booking, such as ground handling partners.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time via our Contact Us page.
