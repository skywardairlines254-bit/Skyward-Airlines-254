The Skyward Lounge

Introduction

A quiet start to your journey

Available at Wilson Airport and Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, the Skyward Lounge offers a calm space to work or relax before boarding — away from the main terminal crowds.

Facilities
  • Complimentary tea, coffee and light refreshments
  • Fast, free Wi-Fi and charging points
  • Quiet seating and workspace areas
  • Dedicated boarding announcements
