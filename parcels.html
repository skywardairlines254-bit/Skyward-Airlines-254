Parcel Services

Same-day carriage between Skyward Airlines destinations for time-sensitive shipments.

Same-day parcel concept

Send it on the next flight out

Documents, samples and small packages can travel on the same Skyward Airlines flight as our passengers, arriving the same day they're dropped off.

Route coverage

Available between any two destinations on our network with a direct route — see our destinations page for the full list.

Accepted items & restrictions

Documents, small packages and commercial samples up to 20kg are accepted. Dangerous goods, perishables requiring refrigeration, and live animals are not accepted as parcels — see our Dangerous Goods Policy.

Process

1. Drop-offAt departure airport, 60 min before flight
2. In transitTravels on the passenger flight
3. CollectionAt arrival airport counter
