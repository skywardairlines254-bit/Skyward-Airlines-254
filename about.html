About Skyward Airlines

Our Story

Built for the routes other airlines skip

Skyward Airlines was founded to connect the towns and coastlines that larger carriers overlook — from Turkana in the north to the Tanzanian border in the south. We started with a single Nairobi–Mombasa route and have since grown to serve 15 destinations across three countries.

Who We Are

A regional airline, run regionally

Our crews, engineers and reservations team are based across our network, not just at a single head office. That keeps us close to the routes we fly and the communities we serve.

Our Mission

To make regional East African travel reliable, affordable and genuinely convenient.

Our Vision

To be the airline that connects every significant town in East Africa within a two-hour flight.

Our Values

Safety first, straightforward service, and respect for the communities along our routes.

Safety

Safety is not negotiable

Every Skyward Airlines aircraft is maintained to manufacturer and regulatory schedules, and our pilots and cabin crew complete recurrent training well beyond the regulatory minimum.

Regional Connectivity

Two hours, not two days

Routes like Nairobi–Lodwar turn a two-day road journey into a flight of under two hours — the kind of connectivity we believe East Africa deserves.

Customer Service

Real people, on the ground and in the air

Our reservations team handles every booking enquiry directly — no call centre scripts, just people who know the network.
